## Introduction

Anthropic has just unveiled **Claude Fable 5** and **Claude Mythos 5**, the newest members of its Mythos‑class family. These models push the frontier of reasoning, vision, and long‑context understanding while introducing a new suite of safety classifiers designed to keep powerful capabilities in check. For teams building autonomous agents, the release signals a shift toward more capable, yet responsibly governed, AI systems.

## What’s New in Claude Fable 5?

- **State‑of‑the‑art reasoning**: Benchmarks show Fable 5 outperforming all previously released models on software engineering, finance, and scientific tasks.
- **Vision prowess**: It can extract precise data from complex figures and even rebuild web apps from screenshots alone.
- **Long‑context memory**: The model stays focused across millions of tokens, improving performance in multi‑step games and simulations.
- **Token efficiency**: Early adopters report up to 30 % faster completion of coding and analytics workloads.

## The Mythos 5 Variant and Trusted Access

Mythos 5 shares the same core as Fable 5 but lifts certain safety constraints for trusted partners in cybersecurity and life‑science research. Access is granted through **Project Glasswing** and a forthcoming biology‑research program, ensuring that high‑risk capabilities are only available to vetted users.

## Advanced Safety Classifiers

Anthropic introduces three new classifier families:

1. **Cybersecurity** – blocks offensive hacking queries, falling back to Opus 4.8 when triggered.
2. **Biology & Chemistry** – restricts potentially dangerous bioweapon‑related instructions.
3. **Distillation** – prevents large‑scale extraction of model capabilities.

These classifiers have been red‑teamed extensively, with early data showing a 95 % session‑level success rate and minimal false positives.

## Implications for Developers

- **Faster prototyping**: Teams can now compress months of engineering into days, as demonstrated by a 50‑million‑line Ruby migration completed in a single day.
- **Responsible deployment**: The built‑in safeguards reduce the need for custom safety layers, letting developers focus on product logic.
- **Cost‑effective scaling**: Pricing at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens is less than half the cost of the previous Mythos Preview.

Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 represent a significant leap forward for agentic AI, delivering unmatched capability while embedding robust safety mechanisms. For organizations looking to harness autonomous agents responsibly, this release offers a powerful new toolset.

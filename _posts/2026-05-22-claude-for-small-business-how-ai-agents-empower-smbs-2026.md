## Introduction
Small and medium‑sized businesses (SMBs) have long been the backbone of the economy, yet they have been largely left out of the AI boom. While large enterprises pour billions into custom AI stacks, many SMBs still rely on manual spreadsheets and ad‑hoc scripts. Anthropic aims to change that with **Claude for Small Business**, a new suite of AI agents and connectors launched on May 13, 2026. The offering bundles Claude Cowork, 15 ready‑to‑run agentic workflows, reusable skills, and deep integrations with the tools SMBs already depend on—QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, Docusign, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and Slack.

## Why Small Businesses Matter for Agentic AI
SMBs represent a massive, underserved market. According to recent data, over 30 million SMBs operate in the United States alone, collectively generating trillions in revenue. Their challenges are unique: limited IT staff, tight budgets, and a need for rapid ROI. Agentic AI—systems that can plan, execute, and self‑reflect—offers a way to automate repetitive tasks, improve decision‑making, and scale without hiring additional headcount. By targeting this segment, Anthropic is not only expanding its user base but also democratizing access to powerful AI capabilities.

## What Is Claude for Small Business?
At its core, Claude for Small Business is a **managed agent platform**. It exposes APIs that let developers define an agent’s responsibilities, tools, memory, and guardrails. Under the hood, the service works tightly with Claude models, supporting tool calling, multi‑step loops, and secure integration with external systems. The package includes:

* **Claude Cowork** – a conversational workspace that can draft emails, summarize documents, and generate code snippets.
* **15 Ready‑to‑Run Workflows** – pre‑built agents for invoicing, lead nurturing, inventory alerts, social media posting, and more.
* **15 Reusable Skills** – modular capabilities such as “Extract PDF Data,” “Generate Invoice,” and “Update CRM.”
* **Connectors** – bi‑directional sync with QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, Docusign, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and Slack.

All of this is offered **without an extra charge** beyond the cost of Claude licenses and the existing subscriptions to the connected tools.

## Real‑World Workflows in Action
### Automated Invoicing with QuickBooks
A boutique design agency can now trigger a workflow that monitors new project milestones in Asana, generates a professional invoice in QuickBooks, and sends it via email—all without human intervention. The agent pulls the latest project details, formats the invoice using a Canva template, and logs the transaction in the accounting system.

### Lead Nurturing in HubSpot
Sales teams can deploy a workflow that watches for new contacts entering HubSpot, enriches them with data from public APIs, drafts a personalized outreach email, and schedules it for optimal send times. The agent learns from open‑rate feedback and refines its copy over time, delivering a 20 % lift in conversion rates.

### Slack‑Driven Customer Support
A SaaS provider can set up an agent that listens to Slack channels, triages incoming support tickets, and automatically creates tickets in Zendesk with all relevant context. The agent can also suggest resolutions based on past tickets, reducing average response time dramatically.

These examples illustrate how **agentic AI** moves beyond simple prompting to full‑fledged automation that respects business rules and data privacy.

## A New Open Frontier for Physical AI

On June 1, 2026, NVIDIA unveiled **Cosmos 3**, the first fully open omnimodel designed for physical AI. Unlike traditional language‑only models, Cosmos 3 natively processes text, images, video, sound, and even robot action signals in a single system. This breakthrough mixture‑of‑transformers architecture gives developers a generational leap in building robots, autonomous vehicles, and vision‑AI systems that can perceive, reason, plan, and act in the real world.

## What Makes Cosmos 3 Different?

- **Omnimodal Capabilities** – One model handles text, image, video, audio, and action outputs, eliminating the need to stitch together separate vision and language components.
- **Two‑Tower Mixture‑of‑Transformers** – A novel architecture that blends world‑generation with action prediction, delivering state‑of‑the‑art physics accuracy.
- **OpenMDW‑1.1 License** – Fully open weights and training tools, enabling commercial use without restrictive licensing.
- **Leaderboard Dominance** – Claims top‑rank positions on more than eight benchmarks for vision reasoning, text‑to‑image, image‑to‑world, and world‑action generation.

## Real‑World Use Cases

- **Robotics** – Pick‑and‑place tasks, warehouse safety monitoring, and closed‑loop simulation for robot control.
- **Autonomous Vehicles** – Synthetic data generation and perception pipelines that improve navigation in complex environments.
- **Vision AI** – High‑fidelity video generation for training perception models and augmenting scarce datasets.

## The Ecosystem Behind Cosmos 3

NVIDIA has assembled the **Cosmos Coalition**, a global collaboration that includes Agile Robots, Black Forest Labs, Generalist, LTX, Runway, and Skild AI. Together they contribute models, research, and evaluation techniques, while leveraging NVIDIA DGX Cloud for large‑scale training. The coalition’s open approach accelerates innovation across industries and ensures that the model evolves with community input.

## Getting Started

The model weights are already available on **Hugging Face**, and a lightweight inference demo runs on **build.nvidia.com** without a dedicated GPU. Developers can experiment with the provided Diffusers integration, explore the curated datasets for physical AI, and tap into the Cosmos Framework for custom pipelines.

<p><strong>Last updated: 22 October 2026.</strong></p>

<p>This policy explains what personal data is collected when you visit gritsa.com or contact us, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short because we collect very little.</p>

<h2>Who we are</h2>
<p>gritsa.com is run by Gritsa Technologies (Noida, India) together with Gritsa LTD (United Kingdom). Either of them is the "controller" of the data described here. Contact: <a href="mailto:info@gritsa.com">info@gritsa.com</a>.</p>

<h2>What we collect</h2>
<h3>Analytics</h3>
<p>We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are read and where visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and receives information such as the pages you view, the time you spend on them, the page that referred you, your approximate location (country and city level), and your browser, device and operating system. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We use this data in aggregate to improve the site. We do not use it to identify individual visitors and we do not combine it with other data.</p>

<h3>Messages you send us</h3>
<p>If you email us or use the contact form, we receive the details you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, company and message. We use them only to reply to you and to discuss work with you. We do not add you to a mailing list without asking.</p>

<h3>Hosting logs</h3>
<p>The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and requested page) for security and operation of its service, under <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement" rel="noopener">GitHub's privacy statement</a>. We do not receive those logs.</p>

<h2>Third parties that load on our pages</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager</strong> (analytics, described above).</li>
<li><strong>Google Fonts</strong>: your browser requests font files from Google's servers when a page loads, which tells Google your IP address and the page's address.</li>
<li><strong>Content delivery networks</strong> (for example cdnjs and unpkg) that serve some open-source scripts and styles.</li>
<li><strong>Links out</strong> to GitHub, LinkedIn, X, Facebook and the TechRxiv research paper. Once you follow a link, that site's own policy applies.</li>
</ul>
<p>Some of these providers are based in the United States, so your data may be processed outside your own country. We do not sell personal data.</p>

<h2>Cookies</h2>
<p>The site does not set tracking cookies of its own. Google Analytics sets cookies such as <code>_ga</code> to tell visits apart. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install <a href="https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout" rel="noopener">Google's opt-out browser add-on</a>. The site works the same without them.</p>

<h2>Why we process data</h2>
<p>We process analytics data because we have a legitimate interest in knowing whether our content is useful and in keeping the site working. We process messages you send us because you asked us to reply, and to take steps toward a contract if you are considering working with us. Where the law requires your consent for analytics cookies, please block them as described above, or write to us and we will help.</p>

<h2>How long we keep data</h2>
<p>Analytics data is kept for the limited period set in our Google Analytics data retention settings, then deleted by Google. Emails and messages are kept while we are in contact with you and for as long afterward as is reasonable for business records, then deleted.</p>

<h2>Your rights</h2>
<p>Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to <a href="mailto:info@gritsa.com">info@gritsa.com</a> and we will answer within a reasonable time.</p>

<h2>Children</h2>
<p>This site is for businesses. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their data.</p>

<h2>Changes</h2>
<p>If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.</p>
